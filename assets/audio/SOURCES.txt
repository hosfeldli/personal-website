Audio assets used by The Operations Dungeon
============================================

arsenal-cz-single.wav
  Production file: a trimmed single-shot edit of arsenal-cz.wav.
  Original source: OpenGameArt.org — Gunshot Sounds
  https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sounds.zip
  License: CC BY 3.0. The downloaded archive includes license text crediting
  Vincent Sevedge. The single-shot edit is used so one trigger press produces
  one report instead of replaying the archive's multi-shot recording.

shotgun-blast.wav
  Source: OpenGameArt.org — Gunshot Sounds
  https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/sounds.zip
  License: CC BY 3.0. The downloaded archive includes license text crediting
  Vincent Sevedge.

shotgun-shell-first.mp3
  Production edit of “First Shell.mp3” from OpenGameArt.org — Shotgun Reload
  Sound effects.
  https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
  The author states that the sounds may be used without attribution.

shotgun-shell.mp3
  Production edit of “Subsequent Shells.mp3” from OpenGameArt.org — Shotgun
  Reload Sound effects.
  https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects
  The author states that the sounds may be used without attribution.

shotgun-rack.mp3
  Original reload/action sample from the same OpenGameArt shotgun source page.
  It remains in the repository for reference; the active reload now uses the
  first-shell and subsequent-shell clips once per inserted shell.

dungeon-ambient.ogg
  Source: OpenGameArt.org — Loopable Dungeon Ambience
  https://opengameart.org/content/loopable-dungeon-ambience
  License: CC0, as listed on the source page. It is used as a quiet cave bed
  beneath the death-metal score.

The game-generated soundtrack uses Web Audio oscillators, waveshaping, bass,
drums, and noise layers. It is intentionally low-tuned, fast, dissonant, and
metal-oriented rather than chiptune-oriented.
