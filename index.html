    THE FIELD MANUAL

    Move, fight, read

    • WASD moves through one connected dungeon. A and D strafe.
    • Move the mouse to look around. Click to attack; hold the mouse button to continue attacking when the recovery window opens.
    • Space dodges in the direction you are moving, costs stamina, and grants a brief invulnerability window.
    • Chain up to three attacks inside the combo window for increased damage. Watch the stamina bar before committing to the third strike.
    • Ranged enemies draw a warning line and reticle before firing. Ground attackers mark a warning circle before the hazard activates. Dodge the telegraph or move clear before the impact.
    • The Wayfarer is the only source of weapons. Approach him in the first room and press E to speak with him. Use W for the spear, A for the crossbow, S for the Ember Wand, and D to cycle. Z and X cycle learned spells; Q casts the selected spell.
    • E talks to the Wayfarer, reads the glowing portfolio scroll or a field record, or opens the archive gate. Defeat the final boss, then reach the door of light and press E to return to the portfolio lobby.
