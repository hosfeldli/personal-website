    THE FIELD MANUAL

    Move, fight, read

    • WASD moves through one connected dungeon. A and D strafe; hold Shift to sprint.
    • Move the mouse to look around. Click to fire with the equipped weapon. Press R to reload.
    • Press 1 for the Assault Rifle, 2 for the Shotgun, or 3 for the Electric BFG; each loadout has its own ammo pool.
    • Every normal sector has an access terminal beside its eastern shield door. Hostiles are optional there: use E, read the portfolio file, and run its computer program to authorize the route.
    • Boss arenas are the exception. Defeat the named boss, then return to that arena’s terminal to release the exit shield. Lesser enemies can always be bypassed.
    • Look for the full-height security shield at a locked entrance. Its terminal tells you exactly what remains and the barrier retracts after authorization.
    • Press Space during a cinematic to return to the action immediately.
    • Wardens pursue you and strike at close range. Keep moving and create distance from their melee attacks.
    • Start inside the dungeon with the Assault Rifle equipped. Press 1, 2, or 3 to switch loadouts, and press R to reload.
    • E uses terminals and opens doors. After the final Archon falls, authorize the Lightwell terminal, enter the ascension gate, and approach the résumé pedestal for an automatic download.
